Poiesis

/ poy·EE·sis /  — to bring into being

The ancients used one word for the poet and the maker, for the verse and the machine: to call something forth from silence, and give it a form that can be known.

Tanmay Joshi
τέχνη

Technē

Before there was code, there was logos — the belief that the world could be reasoned with, that beneath its noise lay a pattern waiting to be named. I build intelligent systems in that spirit: not to replace the mind, but to hold a mirror to it.

Write here about your craft — the languages you think in, the problems in artificial intelligence and computer science that keep you awake, and how the discipline of verse (measure, restraint, the courage of the single exact word) shapes the way you design.
